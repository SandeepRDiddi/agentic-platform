# Incident Response Policy

Severity 1 incidents are outages that affect all customers of the Data Marketplace. For a Severity 1 incident the on-call engineer must acknowledge the page within 5 minutes and open an incident channel in Teams. The Incident Commander is assigned by the on-call engineer and owns all communication until resolution.

Severity 2 incidents are partial degradations. The on-call engineer must acknowledge within 15 minutes. A ticket must be created in the ticketing system and linked to the incident channel.

After every Severity 1 or Severity 2 incident the Incident Commander must publish a blameless post-mortem within 5 business days. The post-mortem is reviewed by the Platform Architecture Board.

# Data Access Requests

Access to restricted datasets in the Data Marketplace requires approval from the Data Owner. Requests are filed through the Marketplace and the Data Owner must respond within 3 business days. Access to datasets containing personal data additionally requires sign-off from the Data Protection Officer.

Access grants expire after 90 days and must be renewed. Data Stewards review expiring grants every month.
